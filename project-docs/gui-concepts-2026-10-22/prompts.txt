COMMAND PANEL FLOW
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high fidelity game GUI concept art sheet for DEFENDER, three wide gameplay screen concepts stacked vertically on a single large landscape presentation board.
Primary request: a clean, distinctly game-like tactical tower-defence GUI that directs attention to the current step and important decisions.
Input image: reference image for the existing game's world and model art only; redesign all interface elements.
Scene: preserve the reference's voxel diorama identity: mossy woodland, porcelain mechanical turrets, limestone route, reflective narrow river, tiny wildlife, distant wooded vista. Isometric battlefield; no people.
Design: sophisticated compact field-command instrumentation, charcoal ceramic panels, warm ivory legible typography, subtle moss/teal signals, one amber primary action. Precisely drawn angular corner cuts, restrained engraved borders, utilitarian condensed headings and highly readable numbers. Strong spacing and hierarchy; no generic web dashboard, rounded SaaS cards, navigation sidebar or spreadsheet tables. No excessive sci-fi ornament, neon holograms or crowded tiny labels.
Composition: show three equally polished wide 16:9 gameplay frames on a dark neutral sheet, labelled LOADOUT, DEPLOYMENT, COMBAT in the gutter. The board occupies most of each screen. Consistent layout and world across all three. Large crisp readable text.
LOADOUT: compact top-left DEFENDER and A2 / BROADWATER; top-right 8 TOKENS and five breach shield pips. Bottom requisition rail with four illustrated 3D weapon silhouettes and concise prices: TWIN GUN 2, ARC 4, MISSILE 4, GENERATOR 4. Highlight one gun. Bottom-right one prominent amber DEPLOY DEFENCES button. Small step progression 1 EQUIP / 2 DEPLOY / 3 DEFEND; EQUIP active.
DEPLOYMENT: same battlefield, selected porcelain gun ghost on a valid green tile, restrained dotted range circle, clearly visible route entrance and exit, compact selected-unit panel with DMG 24 / RNG 72 and RECALL. Bottom rail reduced to purchased units with 5/6 PLACED. DEPLOY active; amber START WAVE button largest control. Small contextual instruction PLACE YOUR LAST DEFENCE above rail; amber focus only here and primary button.
COMBAT: collapse equipment rail entirely to keep action visible. Top-center WAVE 2/4 and segmented wave progress; breach pips upper right, small pause glyph. One brief unobtrusive 12 ENEMIES REMAIN label. Vivid but restrained muzzle flash and enemy combat; no build buttons. Small understated bottom-left performance footer 60 FPS · FRAME 16.7 · CPU 1.2 · GPU 4.6 ms.
Constraints: concept art only, no app browser chrome, no watermark. Existing game art remains the hero. Artwork polished enough to assess a future redesign; all three states have unmistakable focus and strong silhouettes.
