## Candidate and final acceptance questions

Review matched normal-overview captures against the frozen landscape. Follow the entire visible route from each entrance to the exit, including any bridge or shared tail. Check that paving has a different value/shape from sandy ground and that crossings/merges communicate the actual directed route.

Assess every surveyed useful alternative as a full 24×24 ground area. A model's physical clearance, range, LOS and an unobscured site centre do not establish full-footprint visibility. Prioritise default-view silhouettes of nearby mesa caps, walls and cactus arms; use bounded removals or existing low replacements when required. Check bank areas and near/far sides independently. Candidate adjacency does not promise simultaneous rotating-weapon placement.

Record tactical restrictions from Tactician's actual weapon checks and preserve scenery anchoring all retained bird perches. A cleared spawn is insufficient wildlife evidence: require the movement-envelope connectivity and completed-journey audit, as well as a live observation from Lead.
