# Higher Drone filming, depth of field and anticipation

20 October 2026. User-authorised camera refinement, implemented for review. This supersedes the original Drone delivery’s lower heights, purely reactive composition and disabled lens pass. Gameplay, enemy movement, assets, weather and balance remain unchanged.

## What changed

Drone now uses subject-focused depth of field through the existing camera postprocess. Its clean profile preserves original colour, framing and edge pixels: no handheld grain, crop, colour ageing, distortion, fringing or vignette. Cam man retains its existing film treatment. Focus uses subject depth along the actual bounded gimbal axis, rather than radial distance while turning. The foreground and background soften outside a ±12% focus band; blur is capped at four CSS pixels. Translucent water/particles inherit opaque depth; this remains a subtle screen-space effect, not optical bokeh.

Ground-only Drone stations use path+60/96/128, with a preferred horizontal span of 128–140 and a 100–180 useful holding band. Airborne scenes set the minimum to at least prepared/authored flying aim+60 and full reserved lane top+12. Current A2–A4 use Y133/157/181, with transit at least Y157; ground-only A1 uses Y84/120/152. Swept hull clearance, canopy-safe travel and actual actor checks remain. Separation preferences are composition rules, not an assurance of a fixed distance from every future actor in custom scenes. [Controller implementation, exact profile and finite witnesses](ENGINEER.md).

Both cameras share a read-only future-encounter forecast for guns, Arcs and missile launchers. It follows each enemy’s actual complete route, including bends, merges and scheduled arrival, and checks weapon readiness, real range, approximate pitch and required line of sight. An imminent encounter supplies a stable future enemy/defence composition. Both controllers invalidate old movement goals and reposition before range entry if necessary. A clear existing shot remains held; actual visible bursts/recent firing take priority. The helper never calls mutable targeting/eligibility planners or changes damage, readiness, health or poses. It cannot guarantee survival, exact muzzle/contact validity or future target competition.

The user subsequently requests smoother flight/aim, continuous slow movement even while framing a stable encounter, and 20% less maximum Drone magnification. Flight now eases a three-dimensional velocity vector with bounded acceleration and jerk; the gimbal and zoom also ease their velocities. A slow 0.65-unit/s survey drift around a six-unit shot anchor keeps useful compositions moving during waves and settling. Anticipation and the final action focus remain stable while the viewpoint drifts. Pause freezes every motion/lens state. Existing swept gates remain authoritative; an impossible packed scene may stop safely instead of clipping through scenery. The optical zoom cap increases the closest FOV from 42° to 51.266°, with the wider 76° end unchanged, reducing maximum magnification to 80%. Drift/velocity scratch adds 96 fixed typed bytes per Drone controller. Final movement checks are included in the Engineer handoff.

Each controller scans every 0.6 seconds, up to 96 enemy/defence pairs and 37 half-second samples per pair over an 18-second horizon, with a four-second event commitment. Each helper uses 112 fixed typed bytes; no growing prediction cache. Pause freezes pose, lens and forecast. Scene/wave refresh and exit clear old references.
