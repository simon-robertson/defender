Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: polished landscape game defence concept sheet for the existing game Defender, a new design for user approval.
Create a coherent three-stage family of autonomous machine-gun drones with their dedicated landing/recharge pads, in the established Moss & Porcelain arsenal style.

Presentation: warm ivory studio background, elegant dark forest-green serif headings, soft directional studio shadows. High-quality clean isometric hard-surface 3D concept illustration, readable chunky silhouettes, restrained detail, predominantly orthogonal stepped forms that can translate to a texture-free unit-grid game model. Matte clean materials, no grime, painted grain or realistic surface textures. Wide composition with three equally prominent columns labelled exactly "BASE", "UPGRADE 1", "UPGRADE 2". Heading exactly "MOSS & PORCELAIN", subtitle "DRONE DEFENCE". The upper two-thirds show the three full drone-and-pad assemblies at the same camera angle and comparable scale; nothing cropped.

Drone design: a compact low central porcelain-armoured fuselage with dark forest-green mechanisms, moss-green inset panels, four small square shrouded lift-fan pods on short sturdy arms, and a SINGLE forward-facing machine gun on a small aimable underbody mount. Clearly show that gun below the nose and between the landing supports, with an unobstructed muzzle and ground clearance when parked. Squared rotor housings, mechanically plausible separate blocky fan blades, no decorative exposed cables. Four clearly separate fan pods on every stage. No missiles or secondary weapons. Small vermilion-red luminous sensor/accent strips tie it to the existing turrets; keep the pale armour matte and nonemissive. Do not use green glow: green luminous highlights identify enhancers in this game. Keep a friendly chunky industrial silhouette, not a modern military aircraft or insect enemy.

Pad design: low square warm stone foundation with a dark green landing deck, porcelain perimeter sections, small vermilion corner accents, two low docking/contact rails and compact rear charging hardware. The drone rests on its supports on the pad in all three main views. Gun remains clear of the deck; charging contacts align with the drone. Pad and parked drone plausibly fit within one 24-by-24 world-unit placement box; upgrade silhouettes stay within the same square footprint. No hangar walls or roof.

Progression: BASE has a slim dorsal battery housing and simple pad charger. UPGRADE 1 adds visibly deeper paired dorsal battery modules with more structured armour and a sturdier compact charger. UPGRADE 2 uses a taller integrated central energy pack with stepped cooling blocks and a more developed rear charger, while preserving the four fans, single gun, compact footprint and recognisable family. Communicate improved flight endurance through the power-pack progression, not extra weapons. Do not print numerical gameplay stats or duration values.

Lower third: one clean, slightly larger airborne three-quarter study of the BASE drone, separated from a smaller matching empty pad so the machine gun, fan layout and landing supports are clear. Add one simple thin line-arrow loop diagram with three small silhouettes labelled exactly "TAKE OFF", "FLY & FIRE", "RETURN & RECHARGE". It must show return to the same pad. Diagram arrows are editorial annotations, not in-world beams or trails. Small restrained note exactly "Upgrades extend flight time". Show no actual gunfire, enemies, exhaust, smoke, sparks, haze, shields, missiles or terrain scene. No extra text, badges, fake stats, watermarks or logos.

The concept should feel like a high-quality new member of an existing porcelain-and-forest-green twin-barrel turret / square vertical missile-silo family, while its airborne drone and flat landing pad make its function instantly distinct.
