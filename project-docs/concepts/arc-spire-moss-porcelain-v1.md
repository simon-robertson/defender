# Arc Spire electric defence concept

Created on 18 October 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool, following the user's request for a new electric/energy defence that zaps enemies, inspired by Tesla coils. **The user accepted the concept and approved modelling on 18 October.** Arc Spire remains its working name. [Concept sheet](arc-spire-moss-porcelain-v1.png), [complete generation prompt](arc-spire-moss-porcelain-v1.prompt.txt).

Artist proposed an upright square-coil family to distinguish it from the existing twin-barrel turret while retaining the Moss and Porcelain material palette. The sheet shows **Base**, **Upgrade 1** and **Upgrade 2** at a common viewing angle: a dark central mast, separated porcelain insulators, moss support ribs and a square discharge crown. Height and reinforcement increase through the family while the mounting pads retain a common footprint. Pale blue-white electrical arcs illustrate the requested energy theme.

Lead and Artist inspected the sheet for three complete variants, correct labels, coherent palette, visible construction and distinct upgrade silhouettes. Artist found no blocking image artifacts. It is a visual proposal, not a dimensioned or validated model. Illustrative bevels, tapered supports, exact 24-unit X/Z clearance and play-scale proportions would need to be resolved during separately approved grid modelling. Structural materials should remain matte and texture-free.

The follow-up authorises the three models and red identity accents at maximum emission (1) and opt-in local lighting (`castsLight: true`), using the existing turret lighting system. The [models and isolated review](../arc-spire-model.md) are complete and were approved by the user on 18 October, authorising second test levels for A–D and their Shift+number selection controls. Electrical attacks and gameplay statistics remain undefined. Range and attack strength must be explicitly specified before combat implementation; cadence, line-of-sight policy, targeting and any additional effects or abilities remain undecided. The image does not assign chain lightning, stuns or multiple-target attacks.
