## Baseline and early guidance

Inspected [accepted path-free overview](../landscape-reset-2026-10-18/screenshots/moss-03.png), Lead's [fresh untouched overview](screenshots/baseline.png), active `moss-03` source, [landscape metadata](../landscape-reset-2026-10-18/a-metadata.json) and the existing generator. Cypress Rise's defining contrast is a shallow winding stream against tall cypress knolls, with lower orchard groups and separate limestone fragments. It should remain visually distinct from A2's large lake and long central bridge.

The stream comprises 19 wet boxes: a narrow northern entry, repeated bends around X12–14, and an eastward outlet around Z10. Water is Y22 above Y20 bed. Preserve its footprint, bed, water level and connected stepped reading. A short optional wooden crossing can serve the journey without needing to become another centrepiece. No crossing or merge is mandatory.

Existing landmark groups (coordinates `[X,Z]`) offer different places to visit:

- Northern masonry: pillar `[8,4]`, walls `[9,4]`/`[8,5]`, rock `[10,5]`.
- Southern/eastern masonry: wall `[13,11]`, pillar `[14,12]`, shrub `[13,12]`.
- Cypress accents/knolls around `[2,4]` and `[3,8]`; low eastern cypress `[18,5]` and rock `[11,3]`.
- Bird-perch trees `[8,1]`, `[17,12]`, `[7,13]`, which must keep supported anchors for the retained six cosmetic wildlife actors.

Early guidance sent directly to Tactician: compare journeys through the different dry regions and existing landmarks, keeping the stream visually continuous. Avoid stretching route length through long parallel road/stream strips. Bridge and merge options should earn their place through a clearer sequence or different useful choices; neither is required by level number. Use existing art and terrain.

Normal overview crowns and tall cypresses may conceal ground behind them even with physical support and firing LOS. Full 24×24 alternative areas must be visible independently of these checks. Judge scenery removals by useful ground and whole composition, without an arbitrary canopy-preservation quota or candidate count. No actual enemies or defences belong in the current layout preview.

## Comparison status

Tactician's initial directions compare a western-return single journey with a short bridge/eastern loop against separate western/northeastern approaches merging into a southern-meadow shared tail. Artist raised no initial composition objection. For the merge, preserve a legible dry bridge landing and a clear junction so the crossing does not look like an ambiguous fork; extra route count is not itself a visual benefit.

Artist subsequently inspected both proposal sources/manifests and Lead's actual renderer captures at matching overview framing: [Single Western Return](screenshots/single-west-return.png) and [Garden Confluence](screenshots/garden-confluence.png).

| Alternative | Independent visual assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Garden Confluence — recommended** | The western masonry/meadow and tall-cypress side read as distinct approach settings. They join at a simple dry-land T, with the one-box timber crossing separate enough from the junction to avoid implying a water fork. The southern shared ground and second ruin setting give the journey a clear final region. More open land is justified by visibly different branch/shared choices while unequal grove masses still frame play. |
| **Single Western Return** | A simpler, attractive sequence, with a quiet short stream bridge and legible eastern return. The long journey reads as one chain of rectangular courts, however, leaving more of the mainland detached as scenery. It offers less visual distinction between successive settings than Confluence. This is a valid alternative, not a technical failure. |

Both retain the connected stepped stream, recognisable pale masonry and the vertical rhythm of tall cypresses. Garden Confluence engages those existing features more strongly; the recommendation is not based on path/plot counts, route topology alone or total scenery retention. Its shorter span stays subordinate to the stream/landscape, distinguishing A3 from A2's long central lake crossing. No new art, bridge detail or effects are requested.

No full-placement-ground visual blocker was found in Garden Confluence's supplied normal overview. Clearings appear open and distinct, including alternative areas beside the approaches and shared tail. This image review does not establish exact zero occlusion over every candidate polygon, individual weapon envelopes or actual firing opportunities; those require the separate geometric/tactical audit. Route direction at the merge is explained by the proposal's complete directed routes, rather than by new signs or effects.
