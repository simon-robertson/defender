# Tactician balance review — 21 October 2026

**Subsequent update:** the user authorised the Arc retry identity correction, now [implemented and checked](../arc-retry-identity-2026-10-21/README.md). This audit and its source hashes describe the pre-fix state; no numerical balance change was made.

Research and audit only. No campaign tuning, runtime, assets or active browser session changed. Current values remain provisional for user review. This report evaluates the implemented single 4-token/+25% generator, one-hop upgraded Arc chain and 180-unit/s attack flight against the actual retained-wallet campaign.

## Recommendations for the lead

1. **Correct the Arc retry identity issue before numerical balancing (high confidence).** The independent [mechanics audit](mechanics.md) reproduces cooldown ownership moving between enemies when the active-target array compacts. Current replays faithfully include that behavior. A correction is proposed; no runtime fix is delivered here.
2. **Keep the present prices, income and primary damage for the next human review; avoid a blanket compensation nerf or campaign HP increase (high confidence about the evidence limit).** Three substantially different continuously funded portfolios clear, but a gun policy and a policy that stops funding after B fail. Chains remove a particular C3 bottleneck. These witnesses establish viable alternatives and pressure, not a measured human win rate or evidence that every wave is easy.
3. **Treat the generator as a deliberate upgraded cluster purchase, and assess practical recipient count before price changes (medium confidence; pressure comparison below).** Its useful value depends on recipient output, physical firing windows and court geometry. Four active neighbours is a cap, not a typical guaranteed configuration. Do not price every generator as four continuously firing weapons or dismiss it using incidental single-neighbour placements.
4. **Do not interpret the faster missile command as +50% weapon DPS (high confidence for the paired witness).** The unchanged magazines/reloads and real committed flight still dominate. The frozen mixed witness actually makes more enemy contacts with the previous slower attack settings. Preserve the user's requested flight presentation while reviewing actual admission/contact failures before attributing difficulty changes to raw speed.
5. **Review the C3-to-D difficulty shape with human observations before a tuning candidate (medium confidence).** C3.3/4 is the reproducible hard ground bottleneck in the Arc ablation and stopped-investment policy. The same surviving armies handle D after continued purchases. A future bounded experiment should target the reported weak stretch, preserving C3's existing pressure, instead of uniformly raising all enemy health. No numerical candidate has been tested or authorised in this report.

## Generator courts and equal-budget decisions

The original requested full four-neighbour mixed cross was a failed placement diagnostic: from B onward the selected four longest-range recipients could not simultaneously occupy the chosen cardinal court. Its generator remained in inventory. Those files are explicitly **not generator combat evidence**.

A separate complete 320-cell-per-level survey uses production visibility/support/route/scenery/model checks for every legal base-generator centre. Simultaneous homogeneous Upgrade II witnesses reach four neighbours in A1, and four Arcs/missiles in A4; they reach at most three in every B/C/D map under the fixed cardinal subset order. A2 allows two guns or three Arcs/missiles; A3 allows three. This does not prove mixed-family four-neighbour impossibility: the survey is bounded to homogeneous family/tier packages and a fixed subset order. It certifies legal geometry, not useful attacks, wildlife habitat or unlock availability. In particular, the hypothetical Upgrade II packages surveyed on A1 are not actually unlocked during A1; do not treat those geometric witnesses as an affordable opening strategy. The actual replay then performs full deployment preparation and physical combat.

The revised policy tries the two, three and four highest nominal-DPS weapons, all legal centres, and all cardinal assignments, ranking their actual route exposure weighted by nominal output. It reserves the generator's centre in the no-generator budget control as well. This is a bounded purposeful cluster search, not an optimal strategy solver; range coverage is a ranking heuristic and all damage remains actual.

The generator policy saves at B1.1, buys its 4-token generator at B1.2, and deliberately boosts two carried Arcs and a missile. The control spends the corresponding opening 4 tokens on two gun upgrades and a base Arc. Both have spent 35 by B1.2. Later autonomous purchases can differ by token denominations and inventory order; report actual spent/unspent values rather than calling those later armies identical.

At B1.2–4 the generator contributes 3,722.875 / 4,192.8125 / 4,689.6875 marginal useful HP on its observed contacts. This subtracts what an unboosted pulse would have removed from the same pre-hit health. It does not count changed later targets, prevented escapes or extra useful firing windows; the frozen-placement no-generator run below measures their combined effect.

Nominal output explains why recipient quality matters. Four Upgrade II guns add about 47.30 DPS in total for the generator's 4 tokens; three Upgrade II Arcs add about 83.10 direct DPS before chains; two Upgrade II missile launchers add 108 DPS before flight losses. Buying or upgrading another weapon also changes range, firing windows and court availability. A simple “16 tokens of recipients breaks even” heuristic is only a rough linear-output assumption, not a general price proof.

The full-budget generator/control armies both clear all 64 waves for 127/126 spent, with 3/4 tokens left. There are two or three deliberate recipients through B, then three in C/D. The observed marginal useful HP contribution totals 59,179.8125 in B, 71,319.25 in C and 99,968.25 in D. Compared with the control, full-zone resolution times are lower by about 66.9 / 28.3 / 61.3 seconds across B/C/D, although different later portfolios and target feedback prevent attributing all of that to the bonus.

After both policies stop purchases at the end of B, the generator army has spent 64 versus 63 for control, with the same 66 tokens earned at that point. The generator army's first escape is a C1.4 Tripod with 39.5 HP; it clears C3 and fails at C4.4, 47 waves completed. The control first leaks two C1.4 Tripods, then six at C3.3 and reaches defeat at C3.4, 43 waves completed. The generator package therefore buys meaningful pressure headroom in this comparison while still requiring later investment for campaign survival. The single extra token actually spent and changed package/placement mean this policy contrast alone cannot establish that the generator strictly dominates every equal-cost alternative.

The frozen full-budget generator army with the bonus disabled also completes 64 waves, but incurs two C1.4 Tripod escapes (164.5 and 1,244.75 HP remaining) versus zero current. Purchases, 127 spent, variants, legal placements and generator geometry remain identical. The generator's useful bonus, rather than an extra weapon purchase or freed court, prevents these two escapes. With the bonus disabled, total wave-resolution time increases by about 72.45 seconds in B, 76.00 seconds in C and 79.23 seconds in D. Feedback changes later targets and flight outcomes, so this does not isolate only the per-contact HP arithmetic.

Together, these results support keeping the provisional 4-token price for the next review: it is a meaningful optional late investment, helps with real pressure and does not bypass the need for continued funding. They do not establish final fairness, a uniquely best purchase, or a reason to increase its price. Buying a generator early also forgoes immediate attacking purchases/upgrades; no early-generator opening was simulated here.

## Method, provenance and limits

- Actual `PlaySession`, `PlayEngine`, `loadPlayWorld`, physical missile pool, current aiming/LOS/surface contacts and finite authored schedules run with 120 Hz simulation and 30 Hz outer updates. Cosmetic deployment preparation uses the existing safe wildlife path. No rendering/GPU/browser run was performed.
- Arc xorshift starts at 1,234,567 for each production combat coordinator; missile LCG starts at 7,919; gun random returns 0.5. Production controller rebuild/reset behavior is retained. Exact transactions, tiers, legal boxes, per-wave HP/damage/secondary/generator/economy and source hashes are embedded in each JSON.
- `audit.js` is the frozen first runner, derived from the existing production campaign audit. `audit-adaptive.js` is a separate frozen revision for feasible court assignment. Each hashes itself and its predecessor; old outputs are not retroactively attributed to the new policy.
- The source list includes config/session/engine/placement, chain/enhancer/weapon/missile controllers and shared definitions, enemy models and level sources. Hashes are asserted again at run end. This is a scoped source check, not a claim that every repository file was exhaustively hashed.
- Damage is recorded at the actual callback and clamped by current health. Secondary context is instrumented around the real `chain()` call. Reserves are derived from actual arranged deployments before combat, avoiding the historical post-`finishWave()` position-clearing reporting error.
- Successful complete waves reconcile useful damage with scheduled HP. Defeat stops at the miss threshold; remaining pending/living actors are not counted as killed or escaped. Wallet arithmetic is asserted from completed waves and actual spending.
- Placement ranking is deterministic and bounded. Fixed preferred positions and route-radius ranking are not optimal play. The adaptive cluster is purposeful but also not exhaustive. No human reaction/misclick, readable targeting decision, manual action-camera difficulty, skill distribution or GPU performance is simulated.
- Numerical modifiers, current engine quirks and map constraints compose. The independently reproduced Arc retry issue can affect measured firing opportunities. Correctness fixes should precede any confident fine tuning.

The eleven principal current instrumented policy/feature witnesses enter 638 waves; 635 fully resolved waves pass HP conservation and three defeat waves stop early. Every witness passes exact wallet reconciliation and rechecks its recorded source hashes. The two initial failed four-spoke diagnostic campaigns and the parent-owned stopped-after-B witness are separate evidence. No full test suite was run. [Compact aggregates and verification counts](summary.json) are regenerated by `python3 docs/balance-review-2026-10-21/summarize.py`.
