Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished original enemy model concept sheet for DEFENDER, the desktop tower defence game. Landscape 4:3 canvas, ideally 2048x1536, a carefully spaced TWO-BY-TWO grid of FOUR large separate model presentations with consistent three-quarter views and equal visual scale. Each panel gets an exact zone heading above and working-name label beneath. Crisp restrained typography, polished design-document presentation. Neutral warm off-white or very light grey background, soft contact shadows, no scenery, no dark vignette, no texture/noise. Large models fill most of each panel; thin understated panel separators, generous margins. All four labels must be legible and correct.

Game art: distinctive mechanical machines made of chunky solid axis-aligned grid-authored blocks and stepped forms, not pixel-art sprites. Purposeful primary masses, clean component gaps, plausible rigid articulation, mostly matte flat-colour surfaces. No textures, weathering, damage, glossy metal, organic creatures, humans or characters. Refined lighting and ambient occlusion for higher-quality concept presentation. Future models must plausibly fit a 24-unit wide/long grid footprint, with taller silhouettes allowed. Fine bevels can be extremely restrained, but avoid rounded blob bodies, tiny noise or intricate greebles. Use modest glowing sensor eyes as the ONLY luminous accents; no attack effects, guns, launchers, drills, shields, ability symbols, trails, smoke or exhaust. Designs are new enemies, not player defence buildings. The established mechanical Dog quadruped, tall two-leg Walker and four-leg Spider already exist: do not redraw those silhouettes. No ground quadrupeds or spiders.

Panel order and palette: A top-left, B top-right, C bottom-left, D bottom-right.
A: "A · MOSS & PORCELAIN" — warm porcelain upper armour, dark forest mechanisms, restrained moss panels, very small vermilion sensor. Strong pale mass against eventual green foliage.
B: "B · OCHRE & PETROL" — deep petrol dark foundation, sand/ochre armour, small turquoise sensor. Not all ochre.
C: "C · ICE & EMBER" — navy/slate major forms, selective ice-white plates, small ember-orange sensor. Dark edges for snow contrast.
D: "D · PLUM & MINT" — neutral charcoal mechanisms, plum/mauve casing, sparse mint plates, small coral sensor. Clear neutral value separation.
Do not put invented stats, combat class claims, upgrade arrows or game-mechanic annotations anywhere. Deliver one beautiful coherent sheet, not a browser screenshot or photo of a sheet.
