# Zone B route comparisons

19 October 2026. These are original journeys over the accepted frozen B landscapes. Lead and Artist compared the same-scale maps and production-renderer views before production integration. **The selected sources are implemented and were subsequently accepted by the user on 19 October.** The comparison below records the selection process; its geometry and measurements are unchanged. Route length and plot counts are diagnostics, not design quotas or balance claims.

Every option preserves the 20×16 footprint, original terrain, water and environment. Only route endpoints touch the boundary; all intermediate boxes are interior. The final options have no nonconsecutive paved joins that could imply a shortcut. Surveyed full plots are independent authoring alternatives, not fixed build pads or a promise that adjacent rotating weapons can coexist.

## B1: landscape variety around the small cistern

Cistern and Mesa Circuit gives the early western court, northern return, eastern mesa and independent southern catch different roles. Southern Mesa Return preserves more props and relates more closely to the southwestern raised island, but keeps the cistern/northern aqueduct remote and spends longer along the east. Lead and Artist preferred the first journey's landmark variety and clearer sequence.

The selected carve keeps both raised islands and the tiny residual cistern. Three of four original walls remain; `[5,4]` is removed specifically to expose the full alternative plot `[4,5]`. Some northern plots were rejected because original terrain or a retained perch obscured them; their unnecessary footprint removals were restored. All 39 final plots have measured base Arc opportunity against every B species; 38 also have an admissible useful base gun. The short base-range gaps and the turret restriction at `[10,13]` are recorded in the [coverage](cistern-mesa-circuit.coverage.json) and [clearance](cistern-mesa-circuit.clearance.json) reports.

## B2: one deliberate river transition

Riverfold Crossing curls through a western court, crosses the narrow river waist and then gives the eastern bank a northern court, eastern return and southern catch. It preserves all six aqueduct fragments. Its two-box timber deck spans `[6,6]` and `[7,6]`; water and bed remain underneath. Aqueduct Confluence offers separate branch investment, but removes more northern cactus mass and adds a shorter northeastern approach without a stronger scenic journey. The selected single route makes the two banks and crossing easier to read.

The western inner plot `[4,8]` has actual base-gun Walker windows `[54,138]` and `[198,282]`, separated by 60 units: 3.33 seconds at the Walker's 18 units/second. This is a genuine exit and return to range. Nearby `[4,7]` shifts the windows to `[48,114]` and `[222,306]`, trading timing against a longer gap. The eastern return and late court supply separate later investment. The bridge leaves a measured 12-unit base gun/Arc gap for the representative Walker; upgrade-1 coverage closes it. [Actual intervals](riverfold-crossing.coverage.json).

## B3: branch choices and a shared southern circuit

The longer single Three Mesa Tour reaches more northern ground but spends 1,344 units on one journey and leaves four of its 30 plots dependent on ideal upgraded range. Mesa Confluence provides materially separate western and eastern early ground, then a shared circuit around the southern mesa and a southwest catch. The final eastern approach is `[19,5] → [18,5] → [18,7] → [17,7] → [17,9] → [16,9]`; the common tail begins at `[16,9]` and is 552 units long. The unequal complete lengths remain an authoring fact, not a wave-timing decision.

The initial merge sketch removed all three walls. The selected revision routes to the east of `[16,7]` and omits redundant or obscured plots `[7,10]`, `[9,11]` and `[16,8]`, retaining every original wall and bird perch. At actual base eligibility, 23 plots cover the western route only, four the eastern route only, ten both complete routes, and one needs upgraded gun/Arc reach. A plot covering both complete routes may do so on the shared tail; this classification does not imply both exclusive branches are in range.

The retained scenery and upgrade benefit are explicit trade-offs. Representative Walker base Arc coverage leaves main-route `[810,894]` uncovered, while base guns leave `[810,942]`; the corresponding eastern-route windows are `[474,558]` and `[474,606]`. Upgrade-1 Arc covers both complete routes. Lead retains this geometry for user review: continuous base coverage is not a requirement, and no final campaign balance is claimed. [Full species/stage intervals](mesa-confluence.coverage.json).

## B4: the broad lake stays dominant

Aqueduct and Shore Tour follows the eastern mainland around the aqueduct, returns beside the indented lake shore and reaches a separate lower court and southeast catch. Basin Confluence has early branch-specific choices, but 19 of its 29 ideal base-range plots cover both complete routes, concentrating investment and making a broad parallel road band on the constrained mainland. The calmer single tour preserves the western lake silhouette.

Omitting the isolated plot `[14,7]` restores the aqueduct fragments at `[15,6]` and `[15,7]`; all four original walls now remain. Three surveyed shore plots `[10,6]`, `[11,6]`, `[12,6]` need upgraded gun/Arc reach. Twenty-nine others have positive base Arc opportunity against all B species. The retained aqueduct/terrain leaves a representative Walker base Arc gap `[234,294]`; upgrade-1 Arc closes it. No bridge or water alteration is needed. [Actual coverage](aqueduct-shore-tour.coverage.json).
