## Implemented behaviour

`shared/drone.js` retains cruise72 units/s, launch/descent48 units/s, acceleration96, braking120, curve acceleration84 and corner radius18. Body yaw is limited to148.5°/s with405°/s² acceleration. These are provisional preview values. The body stays level: its matrix contains yaw only, with exactly zero pitch/roll. Per-fan collective tilt follows horizontal acceleration in the body frame; differential tangential tilt drives yaw and reverses during yaw braking. Translation retains70% and yaw30% of the shared cone before compound clamping. Fans ease at10.5/s and spin continuously in opposing diagonal pairs.

Artist's shared contract is a20° combined pitch/roll cap, minimum steering lift6, front pivotsY11.5/rearY10.5. Fans stay upright over the pad and during neutral vertical phases; pad alignment uses differential yaw and waits for near-neutral gimbals before descent. The16.5-unit navigation sphere encloses all approved fan and gun poses. Models and level sources were not edited by this controller revision.

`engine/drone-navigation.js` searches the existing bounded four-layer graph, shortens a path only when its complete swept hull is clear, and rounds corners only when the entire expanded curve box is clear. Otherwise it retains a deliberate stopped corner. `engine/drone-motion.js` uses exact quadratic arc length and continuous tangents, acceleration/braking and curvature limits. Completed zero-speed joins are excluded from later limits; mandatory stops clamp exactly at their join. Motion consumes the supplied delta through at most64 subdivisions; flight, recharge and weapon clocks advance once.

`game/drone-preview.js` now chooses **future firing stations** using the target's actual complete route, without a two-second lead cap or attack prediction across exit. A coarse bounded scan produces12 candidates. Only this shortlist gets full route rounding, a dry run of the actual acceleration/braking controller, arrival/aim-settle allowance and certification of up to ten future firing pulses. Range, gun pitch, both static muzzle rays and the target's future physical approach must remain useful. Three useful pulses normally suffice; a finishing shot or urgent near-exit enemy can justify a shorter window. These thresholds and score weights are provisional drone-only tuning.

The drone commits to its selected destination while travelling and holds a useful station while firing. It replans for death/lap change, unsafe geometry, exhausted usefulness or a materially more urgent threat; the provisional challenger threshold is2×. A living current target gets preference when replacing an unusable station. A held station that loses current range or gun pitch is invalidated, including a target passing underneath. An invalid partial burst is cancelled by the existing weapon cycle; cooldown remains five seconds from the last **actual** shot and advances during repositioning. A fresh burst starts only after cooldown, safe arrival, actual alignment/range/LOS and before endurance expiry. There is no new burst continuation rule.

Selected station volumes and relevant home approaches are spatially reserved against the other aircraft's destination, independently of its current hull or damage promises. Return first brakes along the existing safe route. A temporarily occupied return corridor causes bounded retries or an upper passing lane. Reaching an empty held plan is insufficient to descend: actual aircraft XYZ must be at its home node before pad alignment, preventing a false return completion/position snap.

## Collision ownership and lifecycle

`game/defence-preview.js` refreshes drone collision data once before the existing shared planner. The application binds live wildlife once through `drones.setWildlife(wildlife.states)`. The obstacle module caches current posed part bounds and conservative future articulation/translation for living enemies, wildlife and non-drone solid defences. Its separate entrance region anticipates preview wraps without inventing a swept teleport corridor; attack forecasts still stop at exit. See [the obstacle handoff](OBSTACLES.md).

Static terrain/scenery/pads, future actor corridors and current/predicted peer hulls constrain navigation. Every actual movement substep validates its current swept hull. An incoming actor can interrupt a held firing station, launch, alignment or descent. A bounded six-axis escape prefers a safe vertical/lateral lane. Each candidate is checked against time-matched actor envelopes in ten contiguous0.1-second intervals over the next second, then rechecked during the escape. This fixes the observed bird crossing an initially clear ascent midway. Interrupted escapes brake/replan instead of becoming permanently held. Future-occupied paths remain stopped at zero speed rather than creeping forward on alternate frames. Pad-hover avoidance with active targets starts normal launch/endurance accounting.

The existing emergency stop remains a final fallback. This is conservative local avoidance, not a general physics/crowd solver or a proof against every possible future random actor decision. Wildlife retains its existing cosmetic status and movement. Weather, smoke/effects, debris and missile projectiles are not navigation obstacles; collision damage/pushing is not introduced. Future articulation boxes can contain empty space and may cause extra waits or higher routes. Long-range station forecasts cannot predict later wildlife scares; the local snapshot and escape checks handle observed changes.

Approved20-second flight from launch excluding return, ten-second recharge,10HP rounds, ten-round bursts at4 rounds/s and five-second gun cooldown are unchanged. A valid active burst finishes on expiry, then return begins without a new burst or cooldown wait; unsafe shots are never forced. Upgrades, costs and generator interaction remain open. Reset clears flight/weapon/commitment/evasion state and reuses all buffers; disposal releases obstacle/rig/state references. One shared defence candidate thinker remains active per frame, within the existing single RAF loop.
