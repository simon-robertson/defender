Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one polished enemy-design concept sheet for Defender, an existing desktop tower-defence game. These are proposals for review, not engine screenshots.
Primary request: four different, memorable ground enemy machines, one for each established zone. Preserve the family resemblance to a small squat mechanical dog with dark articulated legs, a pale stepped protective shell and a small warm sensor. Do not redesign that dog; invent four distinct complementary silhouettes.

Layout: large landscape board, approximately 3:2, clean ivory background, generous margins, four equally developed panels in a 2-by-2 grid. Title at top exactly "ENEMY DESIGN PROPOSALS". Each panel has one large elevated three-quarter character view, with all feet visible and fully inside the panel, plus a small top-view silhouette inset on a faint square footprint guide. A clear readable working-name caption and zone subtitle. Equal scale reference between panels. No scenery or game HUD. Small footer exactly "Visual concepts · mechanics undecided".

Style: premium stylised 3D game concept art with crisp sculptural geometry and thoughtful mechanical construction. Matte, completely texture-free surfaces: broad flat colour areas, purposeful stepped edges, real seams between articulated rigid parts and readable joints. Axis-aligned box/quad-authored parts; limbs may rotate around joints. Strong large forms with a few restrained secondary details, no random greebles or voxel noise, no smooth round spheres or organic flesh. Soft clear studio directional lighting and simple contact shadows. Concept art can be more polished than exact engine graphics but should look feasible to build with 1-unit cells. No fog, haze, bloom, gloss, weather, particles, smoke, weapons or action effects.

Upper left: caption "A · BRAMBLEBACK", subtitle "MOSS & PORCELAIN". A broad low armoured mechanical beetle, SIX short articulated legs (three each side), stepped segmented protective back and a compact forward vermilion sensor. Dark forest-green shell, warm limestone-coloured central spine, deep charcoal underside, very restrained vermilion. Broad domed-by-steps body, distinct from the dog. Proposed proportions 20 units wide × 22 long × 15 tall. Avoid a completely green silhouette by separating pale spine from dark shell and feet.

Upper right: caption "B · DUNEWALKER", subtitle "OCHRE & PETROL". A narrow tall industrial BIPED, exactly TWO angular reverse-knee legs with broad grounded feet, compact forward upper body on a clear hip assembly. Asymmetric stacked armour detail but a balanced believable gait rig. Terracotta/ochre plates over strongly dark petrol mechanisms, a few warm neutral armour faces, a small turquoise sensor. Proposed proportions 13 wide × 18 long × 27 tall. The tall slim outline should be unmistakable from overhead, very different from a squat walker or turret.

Lower left: caption "C · CINDERHAUL", subtitle "ICE & EMBER". A wide low FOUR-legged heavy machine. A substantial horizontal angular central body with a heavy forward chest, visible load-bearing hip/knee joints and broad feet. The silhouette is solid, compact and powerful, not a beetle or biped. Dark slate/navy main mass, small ember-orange frontal armour panels, ice-neutral joint covers. Proposed proportions 23 wide × 20 long × 18 tall. Keep most of the body dark so it remains distinct against snow and ivory player defences. No gun barrel, cargo payload or implied special ability.

Lower right: caption "D · SPINDLE", subtitle "PLUM & MINT". A flat low central machine on SIX long folded outrigger legs. Distinct thin angular radial outline with deliberate negative space between limbs, compact pale-grey shell segments over deep aubergine lower mechanisms, small muted coral sensors. Proposed proportions 23 wide × 23 long × 14 tall. Keep legs grounded and folded within the square footprint, with plausible hip/knee pivots. Do not make it mint-dominant: mint identifies the player's defences. Clearly flatter and more open than Brambleback.

Footprint constraint: each complete authored model including legs must fit within 24 units in width and length; height may exceed24. Each top inset should make this limit visually understandable without tiny dimensions or illegible technical text. No stats, abilities, damage values, boss labels, health bars, logos or watermarks. This board should help choose silhouettes and colour identities, not claim implemented game mechanics.
